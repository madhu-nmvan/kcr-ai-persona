# Requirement: KCR-AI Persona Oriented AI System

## Objective

Build a persona-oriented AI system called **KCR-AI**, based on K. Chandrasekhar Rao, Telangana politician and leader of the Telangana statehood movement. The system should understand and reproduce KCR’s public speaking persona, emotional tone, behavior, language style, and voice delivery pattern.

## Core Requirement

When a topic is given, KCR-AI should generate a response that speaks and behaves like KCR in public speeches, discussions, and political situations. The system should capture:

* Telangana Telugu slang and dialect
* Switching between Telugu, Hindi, and English
* Political speech style and public-address tone
* Emotional intensity, confidence, sarcasm, anger, pride, and sentiment
* Telangana movement, people-centric, and self-respect framing
* Repetition, rhetorical questions, pauses, punch lines, and emphasis
* Voice tone, tenor, pitch, rhythm, speed, and delivery style
* Body-language style cues and behavioral patterns observed from videos
* How KCR reacts emotionally and behaves in different situations, topics, and contexts

## Source Data

The primary source is YouTube content containing KCR’s speeches, interviews, assembly speeches, public meetings, Telangana movement speeches, and other public appearances.

## Evaluation Requirement

The main focus is to build a **persona evaluation system**. For every generated response, the system should evaluate whether the text, emotion, behavior, multilingual style, political framing, and voice/prosody match KCR’s persona. If the response does not meet the required persona score, it should be regenerated or refined.

## Target Usage

KCR-AI should support one-on-one discussion, group chat usage, and future text plus voice-based interaction.
